The guide also relates the search to the project location, such as interior or exterior painting. These are practical points to identify before contacting a painting contractor or requesting an estimate.

That kind of preparation can help people explain the work they have in mind and look for a painter whose services match it. The guide is intended as a starting point, not a substitute for discussing a specific project with a contractor.
